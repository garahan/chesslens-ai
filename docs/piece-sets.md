# Piece collections — 0.14

Classic, Modern and Outline are joined by three original vector families:

- Staunton: slender traditional bodies, stepped bases and tall crowns.
- Geometric: angular bodies, diamond pawns and clean architectural rooks.
- Rounded: broad soft forms, rounded bases and larger pawn heads.

Each family defines all six pieces on the same 64px baseline. Kings retain crosses, queens crowns, bishops diagonal cuts, knights a horse profile, rooks battlements and pawns a simple head/body. Finishes remain independent of shape. Previews show king, queen, rook, bishop, knight and pawn together, including both sides.
